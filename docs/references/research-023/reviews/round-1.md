# Independent review of the 0.2.3 research draft

## 1. Verdict: materially-flawed

The empirical motivation is credible: all five split-polish summary rows reproduce, a fresh 96-case run is identical to the supplied result, and every historical gap-table value reproduces under the draft's percentile convention. E1 and E2 are distinct from the repository's existing algorithms, but the design is not ready for preregistration: its general evaluator-equivalence claim fails on valid-input corner cases, and its proposed FW-gap is not a valid gap even for a simple continuous fee-bearing CPMM. E2 also needs explicit global cycle admission, execution-domain accounting, and controls that distinguish new support from additional polishing. Most external claims are accurate, with concrete corrections to the JIT date/denominator, lineage wording, and the now-verified v7.0.5 entries; PRIME-Flow is important omitted prior art. I recommend proceeding with a corrected, narrowly scoped E1 design and making E2 conditional on a repaired mechanism and diagnostic, rather than treating the current document as an executable experiment contract.

Reviewed repository: `95d0744db23d19d14f65eafd8512b05d84ec9858`. Draft and probe bytes are pinned in [SHA256SUMS](/tmp/research-023-review/SHA256SUMS). Public sources were fetched on **2026-10-05 UTC**. Repository files were only read; the pre-existing untracked `.pi/` and `docs/external/` entries were unchanged. No tracker, GitHub mutation, Jupiter API, or binary was used.

### Clarifications on source interpretation

The **100x** caution is substantially correct. Ultra V3 says “100x performance improvements … compared to our sunsetted Metis router,” without defining the metric or giving a reproducible corpus. Calling the comparator pre-v7 is a reasonable **chronological inference**, not an identified source/binary pin, and Brent alone cannot be credited for the factor.

The golden arithmetic is correct: `ln(10^-4)/ln(.618) = 19.1377006684`, or **19.1398878671** for the exact golden ratio contraction. It takes **20 contractions** to cross that bracket-width threshold, plus initialization/incumbent/endpoint evaluations. This is a unimodal 1-D bracket calculation, not an output-error guarantee, global solver complexity, or proof of equal-quality work versus a grid. One function evaluation can require many exact pool quotes.

## 5. Missing items and suggested design additions

1. **Publish a narrow executable contract before the campaign.** Define the exact supported topology, share representation, plan canonicalization, original-incumbent identity, protocol/objective refusal rules and every stop. Distinguish failure to quote a candidate from accounting inconsistency; the latter must be visible. Specify ALL_REMAINING placement or prove equivalent explicit final amounts.
2. **Strengthen the fixtures around observed risks.** Add all-zero and zero-gross plans, A smaller than consumer count, drain-before-zero, mixed positive/zero merge inputs, terminal intermediate dust, incomplete CL coverage, CPMM dust/overflow, LB variable-fee reset and bin crossing, repeated-pool ordering, two initializations of equivalent funding flows, invalid-best/valid-second-best activation, and simultaneous branch deactivation needed to remove a token cycle. Use exhaustive integer oracles for small domains and replay every returned plan independently.
3. **Make E1's solver comparison meaningful.** Give golden and Brent identical endpoints, stopping precision, outer traversal and budget. Measure per-case output/work Pareto outcomes; exact equal final gross is not guaranteed and cannot be selected after looking at results. Freeze interpolation safeguards, invalid-point handling, tie/plateau behavior, reproducible arithmetic and cache keys. The thin-intermediate case warrants a local integer-neighbor or breakpoint control.
4. **Redesign E2's opportunity test.** Declare post-state sequential quoting versus aggregate-flow derivatives explicitly. Register δ and sensitivity checks for dust and token decimals. Compare the approximate label oracle with exhaustive admissible H-hop paths on small fixtures; one label per `(layer, token)` is not an exact oracle at H=4 and can miss smaller prefixes that avoid quote failure even at lower depth. Reuse the repository's existing history/admission lessons. Count rejected, duplicate, non-novel and zeroed branches separately.
5. **Use adequate comparison arms without expanding the project unnecessarily.** Primary: A0, A0+golden, A0+Brent, declared common-cap c100/c200, and a work-matched extra-polish control. Secondary: repair, M4±polish, S4±polish, PS±polish/PS2. CFMM±polish is a scoped optional diagnostic on recovered CP+CL plans; it must not hide fallback failures. Use matched SOR cohorts for cross-router claims and keep expanded domains unranked as pure search quality.
6. **Separate a descriptive experiment from a decision gate.** Replace vague H-work/H-P2 wording with predeclared statistics, or label them exploratory questions without pass/fail. Record improvement counts and size, losses/failures, family/stratum/pool-mix distributions, zero denominators, uncertainty and multiple-comparison policy. State exactly which tuning rule nominates a solver and permits E2. The known tuning effect sizes can motivate that rule, but cannot be presented as preregistered evidence.
7. **Complete the operational ledger.** Charge base search, all oracle/replay calls and diagnostics to one 300k/900-second attempt; retain and publish complete incumbents before risky phases. Register cooperative truncation vs hard-timeout behavior and budget-zero identity. Count logical candidate work and physical CL/LB work separately, preserve all scheduled statuses, and pin complete plans/settings/hash evidence. Include reverse/shuffle/rerun determinism checks without repeating report selection.
8. **Preserve the evidence boundaries.** Use repository percentiles, `previously_exposed` report labels, one frozen report comparison per claim, and L01 for any later speed verdict. Source-pin every source snapshot and any implementation reused, with required notices. A static best-known envelope is useful but has no executable portfolio latency; a future charged portfolio needs its own budget and selection contract. Exact caching and zero-loss fast paths remain valid static follow-ups, while dynamic execution quality and new venue primitives remain out of scope.
